HEALTHVERSE
Product Requirements Document (PRD)
Version 1.0
________________________________________
Product Name
HealthVerse
________________________________________
Product Type
Multi-Agent AI Health Companion
________________________________________
Product Goal
To help users explore and understand their health through AI-powered educational guidance and preventive health insights.
________________________________________
Problem Statement
Many young adults lack accessible, engaging, and understandable health information.
Health information often feels:
•	Intimidating
•	Clinical
•	Difficult to understand
HealthVerse transforms health education into an interactive experience.
________________________________________
Target Users
Primary:
•	Young adults (18–35)
•	Gen Z
•	Wellness-conscious users
Secondary:
•	Families
•	Caregivers
•	Individuals interested in preventive health
________________________________________
Core User Journey
1.	User opens HealthVerse.
2.	Nova welcomes the user.
3.	User explores the body or enters symptoms.
4.	Relevant agents activate.
5.	Agents provide insights.
6.	Nova summarizes findings.
7.	User receives wellness recommendations.

Success Criteria
The project succeeds if:
•	Users understand their health better.
•	The interface feels engaging.
•	Multiple agents collaborate.
•	Users experience a "wow moment."
________________________________________
Safety Requirements
HealthVerse:
•	Does not diagnose disease.
•	Does not prescribe medication.
•	Does not replace healthcare professionals.
Emergency symptoms trigger:
•	Safety guidance.
•	Medical attention recommendations.
________________________________________
Accessibility Goals
•	Simple language.
•	Mobile-friendly design.
•	Beginner-friendly health information.
